Computing and space

Study note 2 of 5 · our own summary, not from TCS

  • Charles Babbage designed the Analytical Engine. Ada Lovelace wrote the first published algorithm for it.
  • Alan Turing described the universal machine in 1936 and proposed the Turing test in 1950.
  • ENIAC (1945, University of Pennsylvania) was an early general-purpose electronic computer.
  • The transistor was invented at Bell Labs in 1947.
  • Tim Berners-Lee invented the World Wide Web at CERN around 1989-91. The internet and the web are not the same thing.
  • Linus Torvalds started Linux in 1991. Guido van Rossum released Python in 1991.
  • ISRO: Aryabhata (1975, first Indian satellite), Chandrayaan-1 (2008), Mangalyaan (launched 2013), Chandrayaan-3 (soft landing near the Moon's south polar region, 23 August 2023).
