Sports firsts and records

Study note 4 of 5 · our own summary, not from TCS

  • Modern Olympics began in Athens in 1896. Pierre de Coubertin is the founder of the modern movement. The five rings stand for five continents.
  • Abhinav Bindra won India's first individual Olympic gold (10 m air rifle, Beijing 2008).
  • Neeraj Chopra won javelin gold at the Tokyo Olympics (held in 2021).
  • India won the 1983 Cricket World Cup under Kapil Dev.
  • Sachin Tendulkar is the only player with 100 international centuries.
  • A marathon is 42.195 km. Wimbledon is played on grass.
  • Argentina (captain Lionel Messi) won the 2022 FIFA World Cup in Qatar.
