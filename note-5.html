Math and art

Study note 5 of 5 · our own summary, not from TCS

  • Ramanujan: 1729 is the smallest number that is the sum of two cubes in two different ways (1^3 + 12^3 and 9^3 + 10^3).
  • Brahmagupta (628 CE) gave rules for using zero in arithmetic. Aryabhata worked on place value and astronomy.
  • Andrew Wiles proved Fermat's Last Theorem (announced 1993, completed 1994-95).
  • Pi is about 3.14159. The golden ratio is about 1.618. 2 is the only even prime.
  • Leonardo da Vinci painted the Mona Lisa (Louvre, Paris). Van Gogh painted The Starry Night (1889). Picasso painted Guernica (1937).
  • Shah Jahan commissioned the Taj Mahal in Agra (completed around 1653).
  • Tip: InQuizitive asks about people behind inventions and why they matter, so learn who, what and when together.
