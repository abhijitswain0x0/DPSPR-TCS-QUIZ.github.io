Engineering marvels and engineers

Study note 3 of 5 · our own summary, not from TCS

  • Gustave Eiffel's company built the Eiffel Tower (1889) and designed the iron framework inside the Statue of Liberty. That is the two monuments on both sides of the Atlantic.
  • Isambard Kingdom Brunel: Great Western Railway, SS Great Britain, Clifton Suspension Bridge design.
  • M. Visvesvaraya: engineer and statesman, Krishna Raja Sagara dam in Mysore. Engineers' Day in India is on 15 September, his birthday.
  • Romans built aqueducts, bridges and arches at large scale. The Pont du Gard in France is a famous aqueduct.
  • In an arch, the keystone is the central wedge-shaped stone at the top that locks the arch in place.
  • Hooke's law: stress is proportional to strain inside the elastic limit. Steel is iron with a little carbon.
  • Golden Gate Bridge opened 1937. Panama Canal opened 1914. Burj Khalifa (Dubai) opened 2010.
