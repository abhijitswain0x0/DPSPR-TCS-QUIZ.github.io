Science: names and firsts

Study note 1 of 5 · our own summary, not from TCS

  • Joseph Lister used carbolic acid (phenol) as an antiseptic in surgery in the 1860s.
  • Alexander Fleming noticed penicillin in 1928.
  • Watson and Crick proposed the DNA double helix in 1953.
  • C. V. Raman found the Raman effect (scattering of light) in 1928 and won the 1930 Nobel Prize in Physics.
  • S. Chandrasekhar showed that white dwarfs above about 1.4 solar masses collapse. This is the Chandrasekhar limit. He shared the 1983 Nobel Prize in Physics.
  • Marie Curie won Nobel Prizes in two sciences: Physics (1903) and Chemistry (1911).
  • Kamala Sohonie became the first woman researcher at IISc after protesting outside C. V. Raman's office (this is the story in the TCS practice quiz).
