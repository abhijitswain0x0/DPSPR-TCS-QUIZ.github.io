TCS InQuizitive Study Hub

Everything TCS publishes that you can study from, in one place. 120 recordings, a practice quiz, and the rules.

Heads up: there are no sample papers, answer-key PDFs, fixed syllabus or study pack on the TCS site. The PDFs there are press clippings. The best prep is the practice quiz and watching past rounds to see what gets asked.

1. Practice quiz

The same five questions as the official practice quiz on tcs.com/inquizitive, with answers taken from TCS's own page. It is small, not a full mock test.

Prefer the original? Open it on tcs.com and scroll down about 5 screens to the section "Get InQuizitive. Test your knowledge." (the page cannot jump there on its own).

2. Sample tests Our own

Five practice tests with 10 questions each, written by us from well-known facts. They are not TCS questions and not official past papers. Use them to warm up.

3. Study notes Our own

Short notes on topics that often come up in quizzes like this. Our own summary, not from TCS.

4. What to study

TCS describes the quiz as technological developments across science, sports, engineering, mathematics and art. That is broad guidance, not a syllabus. Read widely, and watch the 2025 and 2024 finals first to see how questions are framed and how the buzzer rounds run.

5. Rules and format

Eligibility, rounds and dates are on the official registration page. Check it for the current season.
